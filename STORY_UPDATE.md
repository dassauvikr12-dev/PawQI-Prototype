# PawQi customer-story refresh

## The brief

The owner asked for a customer-engaging, visual and audible journey. Their friend's brand narrative is the creative basis: caring is not the problem; keeping a hundred little things together is. Features must be the solution, while Milo stays a supporting guide.

## What changed

The homepage no longer opens by enumerating features. It moves from a recognisable everyday problem to an interactive demonstration, relevant services, memories, and a beta invitation.

1. **Less keeping track. More being together.** A visual opening and two choices: try a simpler day, or watch the story.
2. **You're doing your best.** Scattered prescriptions, chats and dates become one organised sample profile on a deliberate click.
3. **What if it lived in one place?** A transition from the problem into the product.
4. **Try Luna's sample day.** Records, routines and memories are hands-on examples rather than paragraphs of feature descriptions.
5. **You don't have to do everything alone.** Direct, illustrated service entry points; no claim of active booking.
6. **They're family.** Journal imagery and planned community lead into wider life-stage features.
7. **Less time searching. More time being there.** A clear beta handoff after the value has been demonstrated.

The full 26-feature catalogue is preserved. Its detailed wording and release boundaries are unchanged; the new narrative is a proposed marketing interpretation, not a replacement for the underlying product plan. Internal workbooks, budgets and personal information are not published.

## Visuals, interaction and sound

Approved local 3D-style still illustrations are reused, with a new editorial layout, scrapbook-style imagery and product demonstrations. They are not newly generated cinematic video assets. Explicit species selection changes pet imagery and the compact assistant together. Dogs use Milo; Cats use Mili; Fish use Nemo; Birds use Mithu; Rabbits use Popi; Small pets use Tiku.

The visual story is an on-demand six-scene web presentation. Captions always remain visible, a transcript is available, sound is off until selected, and the visitor can pause, replay or skip scenes. It stops on close and pauses when the browser tab is hidden. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Optional narration uses browser/device speech, not a recorded voice actor or live generative AI.

## Walkthrough transcript

Looking after a pet is a hundred little things. You're trying to do them right.

A prescription saved somewhere. A date to remember. A note you want to find when it matters.

PawQi began with a simple thought: bring your pet's records, routines and care journey together.

See the reminder you set. Find the record you saved. Make a little more room in your day.

Explore veterinary care, grooming, training and sitting pathways. Real service availability still depends on the rollout.

Because better pet care starts with understanding your pet better. Less keeping track. More being together.

## Review checklist

Open Watch the story; try Sound on, pause and captions. Try Bring it together. Open each sample-day tab and mark a task complete. Switch Cats and confirm Mili takes over. Explore a service, then use the top-right Emergency option. Search All features for Guardian or Travel.

## Scope and validation

This refresh is a deployable public marketing preview, not a production service launch. It adds no live AI, medical advice, saved customer records, real bookings, payment or dispatch. The existing beta destination is unchanged.

153 assertions passed in a local Chromium fixture, including seven viewport widths, six species, all feature dialogs, literal user-input rendering, story playback and simulated speech-control events. No JavaScript errors were observed. Voice quality and the hosted site were not end-to-end tested in that fixture. GitHub deployment status is checked separately before delivery.
