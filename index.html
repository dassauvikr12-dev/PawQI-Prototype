A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before we begin.

Can I explore without creating an account?

Yes. The story, sample dashboard, feature explanations and external emergency search work without signing in. Your real records and saved routines belong in the signed-in PawQi beta.

Is the assistant a veterinarian?

No. Milo and friends guide you around PawQi. This website uses scripted product explanations, not live AI or medical advice. For health concerns, contact a qualified veterinarian.

Can I book a service here?

Not yet. You can explore a service pathway and continue to the development beta. Real provider availability, payment and confirmed appointments are not offered on this website.

Does PawQi support more than dogs?

The product journey includes dogs, cats, fish, birds, rabbits and small pets. Choose your pet to change the guide and sample experience. Actual care and provider suitability must be checked for the species.
